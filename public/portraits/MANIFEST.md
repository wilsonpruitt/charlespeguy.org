# Charles Péguy Portrait Manifest

All images are in the public domain worldwide. Péguy died at the Marne on 5 September 1914, so any work depicting or made by him entered the public domain in most jurisdictions long ago (life+70 lapsed in 1985 for his own writings; portrait photographs of the period are PD-old).

Sourced from Wikimedia Commons. License tags on each source page: PD-Art, PD-old-100, PD-old-70-expired, or CC-PD-Mark.

---

## peguy-pirou-bearded.jpg

- **Description:** Studio photograph, seated three-quarter view. Young Péguy with light beard and moustache, dark suit, intent gaze. The most widely-reproduced photographic portrait — often used on French editions of the *Cahiers de la Quinzaine* and on book covers.
- **Date:** c. 1900s (period of *Cahiers de la Quinzaine*)
- **Photographer:** Eugène Pirou (Paris studio)
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_P%C3%A9guy_by_Eug%C3%A8ne_Pirou.jpg
- **Direct file:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/Charles_P%C3%A9guy_by_Eug%C3%A8ne_Pirou.jpg
- **License:** Public domain (PD-Art / PD-old-100). Source page link.
- **Dimensions / size:** 1088 × 1705 px · 653 KB

## peguy-laurens-portrait.jpg

- **Description:** Oil-on-canvas portrait. Three-quarter length, Péguy bearded, wearing dark cape/cloak, hand resting on a surface, signature "J.P. Laurens" upper left. The canonical painted portrait of Péguy, held in collections associated with the Centre Charles Péguy d'Orléans.
- **Date:** Painted by Jean-Pierre Laurens (1875–1932), painter and brother of Péguy's friend Paul Laurens; likely posthumous (post-1914) based on Péguy's mature appearance.
- **Artist:** Jean-Pierre Laurens
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jean-Pierre_Laurens-P%C3%A9guy.jpg
- **Direct file:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/10/Jean-Pierre_Laurens-P%C3%A9guy.jpg
- **License:** Public domain (PD-Art, PD-old-auto-expired — Laurens d. 1932, >70 years). Source page link.
- **Dimensions / size:** 448 × 600 px · 50 KB (below 1200px target; this is the largest version Commons holds)

## peguy-painted-closeup.jpg

- **Description:** Painted portrait, close-up of head and shoulders. Péguy wearing his distinctive pince-nez, full beard, balding. Intense, weary expression — Péguy in his late thirties.
- **Date:** Period of c. 1908–1914 depicted.
- **Artist:** Unattributed on Commons (likely the same Jean-Pierre Laurens portrait or a related study).
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_peguy.jpg
- **Direct file:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/Charles_peguy.jpg
- **License:** Public domain (PD-old-70-expired). Source page link.
- **Dimensions / size:** 297 × 367 px · 39 KB (below target; only version uploaded to Commons)

## peguy-military-uniform.jpg

- **Description:** Sepia-toned studio/field photograph. Péguy seated on a rustic chair outdoors, wearing his French infantry lieutenant's uniform (kepi, dark tunic with insignia, riding-style trousers and boots), pipe in mouth, hand resting on knee. Taken in the weeks before he was killed leading his platoon at Villeroy on 5 September 1914 (Battle of the Marne).
- **Date:** Summer 1914
- **Photographer:** Unknown
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles-peguy.jpg
- **Direct file:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a6/Charles-peguy.jpg
- **License:** Public domain (PD-old-70-expired). Source page link.
- **Dimensions / size:** 250 × 400 px · 12 KB (below target; only version uploaded to Commons)

## peguy-1894-young.jpg

- **Description:** Group photograph of École normale supérieure (ENS) classmates, 1894 entering class ("promotion"). Péguy is among the standing/seated students in school uniform. Useful as biographical context for his student years before he broke with the Sorbonne establishment.
- **Date:** 1894
- **Photographer:** Unknown (institutional photo)
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_P%C3%A9guy_en_1894.jpg

### Not located at acceptable quality

- **Pencil/charcoal sketches** — The Centre Charles Péguy d'Orléans is known to hold drawings (Pierre Laurens, etc.), but none have been uploaded to Wikimedia Commons. They are presumably PD but require a separate request to the centre, or sourcing from a scanned biography on Internet Archive.
- **"Péguy à bicyclette"** — The famous bicycle photo (Péguy commuting between his Latin Quarter bookshop and the printer at Suresnes) is reproduced in print biographies (e.g., Géraldi Leroy, Robert Burac) but does not appear to be on Commons, Gallica, or the Wikipedia article. Likely held by the Centre Charles Péguy d'Orléans, not yet digitised under an open licence.
- **Péguy with Charlotte (his wife)** — Not on Commons.
- **Higher-resolution versions** — `peguy-laurens-portrait.jpg`, `peguy-painted-closeup.jpg`, and `peguy-military-uniform.jpg` are all below the 1200px target. They are the largest versions Commons holds; obtaining higher-resolution scans would require contacting the Centre Charles Péguy d'Orléans or the BnF.
