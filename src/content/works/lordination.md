Julien Benda's two-part novel *L'Ordination* — followed in series XIV by *La Chute* — is the story of a young intellectual ordained into the secular priesthood of letters. Benda, later famous for *La Trahison des clercs*, was at this point one of the *Cahiers'* most distinctive prose voices, and Péguy gave the work two full cahiers to itself.
