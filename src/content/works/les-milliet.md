Edited by Paul Milliet from his family papers, *Les Milliet* is an eleven-volume family chronicle that traces several generations of a French bourgeois family through the long nineteenth century — Italian travels, the Franco-Prussian war, the Commune, and the moral and artistic life of provincial France. Péguy ran it across three series of the *Cahiers*, treating it as a documentary counterweight to Rolland's fiction.
