## In English

André Bourgeois was a French socialist journalist of the earliest Quinzaine years. His *Quatre jours à Montceau*, published in cahier II-9 (1900) alongside Mantoux and Guieysse's *Intellectuels et socialisme*, was a reporter's notebook from the mining basin of Montceau-les-Mines — the kind of close-grained labour reporting that the young Cahiers wanted to put in front of their Parisian intellectual subscribers.
