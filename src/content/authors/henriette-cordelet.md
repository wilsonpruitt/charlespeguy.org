## In English

Henriette Cordelet was a French literary critic, one of the small group of women writers whose names appear in the Quinzaine's table of contents. Her cahier VIII-13 (1907), *Swift*, is a monographic study of Jonathan Swift in the moralist-comparatist tradition the Cahiers favoured for their literary work.
