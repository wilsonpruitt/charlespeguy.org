## In English

Henri Michel was a French essayist and traveller of the early Quinzaine years. He contributed *Notes sur la Hollande* to cahier V-7 (1904), in the same issue as the Tharaud brothers' *Moines de l'Athos* — one of the small travel essays the Cahiers liked to set beside their political and philosophical work. He should not be confused with his more famous near-namesake, the constitutional historian Henry Michel (1857–1904), author of *L'idée de l'État*.
