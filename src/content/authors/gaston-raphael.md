## In English

Gaston Raphaël was a French germanist and journalist of the pre-war years, one of Péguy's regular contributors on German political and intellectual life. He gave the Quinzaine three cahiers: *Le Rhin allemand* (IV-19, 1903), an editorial presentation of the German Social-Democratic congress *Le congrès de Dresde* (V-16, 1904), and *Der Professor ist die deutsche Nationalkrankheit* (IX-8, 1907) — a sardonic essay on the German university professor as a national type.
