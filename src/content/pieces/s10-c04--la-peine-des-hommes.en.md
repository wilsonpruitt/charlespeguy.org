## The Toil of Men

*Pierre Hamp*

FOURTH CAHIER OF THE TENTH SERIES

### Champagne Wine

This cahier continues Pierre Hamp's series on labor, "The Toil of Men" (*La Peine des hommes*), with an investigation into the champagne wine industry. Hamp follows the production of champagne from the vineyards of the Marne through every stage of manufacture -- the grape harvest, the pressing, the fermentation in the deep chalk cellars, the riddling and disgorging, the corking and labeling -- documenting the labor of the vineyard workers, cellar hands, and all who toil to produce the wine that adorns the tables of the wealthy.

Written in Hamp's characteristic style -- precise, observant, deeply sympathetic to working people -- the text renders the champagne industry as both an art and a system of exploitation, where the beauty of the product masks the hardship of those who make it.
