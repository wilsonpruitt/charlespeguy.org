## To Our Friends, To Our Subscribers

*Charles Peguy*

*This cahier consists primarily of reference material for Maxime Vuillaume's "My Red Notebooks" series: individual biographical notices, an alphabetical index of proper names cited across the six cahiers of the series, and notes and corrections. The biographical notices cover figures of the Paris Commune of 1871, including members of the Commune, military officers, journalists, and other participants. The alphabetical index provides page references across all six volumes of the Red Notebooks (Cahiers IX-10, IX-11, IX-12, X-7, X-8, and X-11).*

*Readers interested in the full index and biographical apparatus are directed to the Archive.org scan linked above.*
